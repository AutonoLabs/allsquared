II.
The method

A four-move checkmate — where everyone wins.

01 · Draft

A contract, drafted in minutes.

Answer a short brief. Our AI writes a solicitor-reviewed agreement fit for the job — plumber, session cellist, freelance developer — in plain English. No clause-juggling.

02 · Fund

Funds held, never held up.

The client transfers the project total into FCA-backed escrow. The money is ring-fenced — neither party can touch it until agreed work is signed off.

03 · Verify

Milestone proof — not promise.

Hit a milestone. Upload photos, a timesheet, a commit, a deliverable. The client has 72 hours to approve or flag. Silence counts as approval.

04 · Release

Paid. Same day.

On approval the funds release to your account — typically within hours. No invoice chase, no 30-day terms, no excuses. The next milestone continues.

VIII.
Honest answers

What most people want to ask, but don't.

What does "FCA-backed escrow" actually mean?

It means client funds are held in a ring-fenced account operated under the Financial Conduct Authority's client-money rules — the same regulatory framework that protects solicitors' client accounts. Neither AllSquared nor the contracting parties can access those funds until the milestone is approved. If our company ceased trading tomorrow, the money would still be yours.

How is AllSquared different from Shieldpay, Trustshare, or Escrow.com?

Those are pure escrow providers. We're an end-to-end contract, escrow, and verification platform built specifically for high-value UK professional services.

  1. i. Not just escrow — real, solicitor-verified contracts on demand. Competitors hold money. AllSquared drafts, reviews, and secures the whole agreement, start to finish, with SRA-regulated UK solicitors.
  2. ii. End-to-end in one platform — no bolt-ons, emails, or extra tools. AllSquared combines AI-powered, lawyer-signed contracts, client-verified milestones, and FCA escrow in a single seamless flow.
  3. iii. Built for high-value B2B services — not asset sales, not gig work. While others focus on digital goods, property transactions, or generic freelance payments, AllSquared is engineered for big-ticket project work across every professional discipline.
  4. iv. Milestone-based, evidence-driven payment — no more chasing. Funds release on proof — photos, deliverables, commits — not on client whim, not 30-day terms, not "just trust us."
  5. v. Human assurance — zero AI slop, real compliance. Every critical contract is reviewed by UK-regulated legal professionals, not just bots or generic templates.
Are your AI-drafted contracts actually enforceable?

Yes. Every template is drafted under the supervision of SRA-regulated solicitors and tested against current English & Welsh contract law. The AI tailors language and milestones; it does not invent clauses. If anything needs bespoke drafting, the platform flags it and routes you to a partner solicitor.

What happens if a dispute actually occurs?

A structured resolution process begins: 72-hour response window, evidence review, optional mediation through our SRA-partnered panel. Funds remain safely in escrow throughout. Most disputes — in our data and the wider industry's — resolve at the evidence stage, because the evidence exists and both parties can see it. Court is genuinely the last resort.

What size of job is this really designed for?

AllSquared is built for project-based engagements between £10,000 and £1,000,000+ — the range where a late or disputed payment is a genuine business event, not just an annoyance. Below around £2,000 the friction of milestones usually outweighs the protection; at that scale, a plain invoice is fine. We'll say so in the drafting flow if your job looks better suited elsewhere. This is not a Fiverr competitor, and we don't pretend to be.

I'm the client hiring — does this help me too?

Significantly. You pay into a regulated account, not a stranger's. Your money doesn't leave until work you can see is delivered. You get a contract written in English, not jargon. And you avoid the most common bad outcome for commissioning clients: paying a deposit to a supplier who then disappears, restructures, or quietly degrades the work.

Is AllSquared itself a law firm?

No. AllSquared is a technology platform operating within the UK's unreserved legal-services market, as defined by the Solicitors Regulation Authority. LITL — our legal-services layer — connects you with independent, SRA-regulated solicitors who advise you directly under their own professional terms and professional-indemnity insurance. We are the infrastructure; they are the lawyers.

How do I know the solicitors in the LITL network are qualified?

Every solicitor in the network is a verified member of the Solicitors Regulation Authority, carries full professional indemnity insurance, is experienced in contract and commercial law, and has committed to fixed-fee, transparent pricing. You can independently verify any solicitor's credentials on the public SRA Register at sra.org.uk.

Can LITL solicitors represent me if a dispute escalates to court?

Yes. If mediation fails and the matter escalates to litigation, network solicitors can represent you through to court. Litigation work is quoted separately — fixed-fee where possible, otherwise scoped carefully up-front — based on the complexity of the case. You are never billed by the hour without knowing.

What is the upper limit on deal size?

The Per Deal plan supports escrow up to £250,000. The Practice plan raises the ceiling to £1,000,000 per agreement. For larger engagements — main contracts, treasury-grade arrangements, multi-million-pound programmes — the Enterprise tier removes the ceiling entirely with a custom treasury setup. Talk to us.
