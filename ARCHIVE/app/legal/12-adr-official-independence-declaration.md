**I acknowledge and confirm that I understand my obligations as an ADR Official under Schedule 3 of SI 2015/542, including:**

3.1 **Independence (Paragraph 3(a)):** I must be independent and impartial in conducting dispute resolution proceedings. I must not be subject to instructions from AllSquared's commercial management. My determinations are made entirely independently.

    [ ] I confirm my independence

3.2 **Fixed-Term Appointment and Removal (Paragraph 3(b)):** I have been appointed for a fixed term (minimum 12 months) and may be removed from office only for cause, specifically:

    - Incompetence or failure to meet required standards
    - Gross misconduct
    - Breach of confidentiality obligations
    - Persistent loss of impartiality or conflict of interest that cannot be managed
    - Failure to meet requirements under Schedule 3

    I may not be removed on grounds of the outcomes of my determinations or for determinations that are unfavourable to AllSquared or the Platform's interests.

    [ ] I confirm my understanding

    [ ] I confirm this obligation

    [ ] I confirm this obligation

## GUIDANCE NOTES FOR ADR OFFICIALS

**The following guidance applies to completion of both Part A and Part B:**

### Conflict of Interest Definition

A conflict of interest is any circumstance that:
- Creates an actual bias or partiality, OR
- Could reasonably be **perceived** as creating bias or partiality

Even if you are confident you can act impartially, if there is a **perception of bias**, you must disclose it.

### Types of Conflicts to Disclose

- **Personal:** Family relationships, close friendships, romantic relationships, social ties
- **Financial:** Ownership of shares, loans, financial dependence, interest in outcome
- **Professional:** Prior client relationships, ongoing professional ties, referral relationships, joint ventures
- **Reputational:** Public criticism, media comments, published views that could suggest prejudgment
- **Institutional:** Employment by or significant relationship with entities in competition with parties

### Timing of Disclosure

- **Initial Appointment (Part A):** Disclose all known relationships with Platform traders as at appointment date
- **Case-Specific (Part B):** Disclose any conflicts specific to the parties in the dispute, even if not previously known
- **Ongoing:** Disclose any conflicts that arise or come to light during proceedings

### Guidance on "Materiality"

A conflict is **Material** if:
- It would prevent you from acting impartially
- A reasonable person with full knowledge of the conflict would lose confidence in your impartiality
- The conflict cannot be managed by disclosure and party consent

A conflict is **Manageable** if:
- It is remote or historical (e.g., relationship ended 5+ years ago)
- It is fully disclosed and both parties, with full knowledge, consent to your continuing
- Managing safeguards can be put in place (e.g., excluding yourself from certain discussions)

### Removal from Appointment

**Grounds for removal for cause:**
- Gross misconduct
- Incompetence or persistent failure to meet standards
- Breach of confidentiality
- Loss of impartiality (including undisclosed conflicts)
- Failure to comply with Schedule 3

**Grounds that DO NOT justify removal:**
- Determinations unfavourable to AllSquared
- Determinations favourable to one party over the other
- Slow resolution or missed deadlines (provided good-faith efforts made)
- Party dissatisfaction with determination
