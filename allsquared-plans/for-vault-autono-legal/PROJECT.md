# Autono Legal (Australia) — separate matter from AllSquared

> **Status:** New plan 2026-07-05. Autono Legal is the regulated Australian arm under the
> AutonoLabs umbrella. First practice focus: **crypto litigation recovery.**
> Construction/SOPA in Australia is parked indefinitely.

## Vision

Autono Legal is an incorporated legal practice that recovers stolen and scam-lost digital
assets for Australian individuals and businesses — tracing, freezing, and litigating — at
fixed fees, powered by the AutonoLabs platform. In a recovery market notoriously polluted
by second-round scammers, the pitch is simple: **the regulated alternative.** A law firm
with fiduciary duties, PII, and trust-account rules, not a Telegram "recovery expert."

## Why crypto recovery first (not construction)

1. **It's genuinely legal practice.** Freezing orders, preliminary discovery against
   exchanges, proceedings, enforcement — the ILP isn't regulatory overhead here, it's the
   moat. Unregulated competitors structurally cannot follow.
2. **It's the existing edge.** This is Nakamoto Legal / Lex Cryptographia's home turf, and
   the UK cybercrime/USDT matter already proved the core workflow end-to-end: chain tracing →
   issuer freeze request (Tether) → law-enforcement reporting (Action Fraud) → recovery path.
   Productise what was done manually.
3. **The market is large and validated by government data.** Reported Australian scam losses
   were $2.18B in 2025 (+7.8%), with investment scams the top category at $837.7M — and
   crypto the dominant vehicle for them (NASC Targeting Scams Report 2025). Reported losses
   understate reality significantly.
4. **Trust vacuum = distribution.** Official guidance warns victims that recovery services
   are frequently scams and that legitimate ones don't charge deceptive upfront fees. A
   regulated firm with transparent fixed fees converts that warning into a referral engine
   (IDCARE, community legal centres, accountants, exchanges' support teams).

## Regulatory & Structure Notes (LPUL / NSW)

- Stand up the ILP: Law Society notification, authorised principal (existing PC + 6.5yr PQE),
  Lawcover PII, conflicts system. Start paperwork now; UK revenue is not dependent on it.
- Costs design around disclosure thresholds: sub-$750 triage (no disclosure), sub-$3,000
  trace stage (short form). Court phase gets full costs agreements.
- Avoid trust-account load where possible: recovered funds flow **directly to clients**
  (exchange/issuer releases to victim, judgment sums paid to client account); trust only
  when unavoidable.
- No "no win no fee" marketing; conditional agreements drafted properly; uplift capped 25%.
- Brand hygiene: Autono Legal is distinct from Nakamoto Legal / Lex Cryptographia (which
  keeps its crypto-industry advisory work). IP and platform stay with AutonoLabs under licence.
