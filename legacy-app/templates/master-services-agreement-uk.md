## 1. Engagement of Services

{{SUPPLIER_NAME}} (the "Supplier") agrees to provide services to {{CLIENT_NAME}} (the "Client") in accordance with the terms set out in this Agreement. The nature and scope of services shall be as described in one or more Statements of Work annexed to this Agreement.

## 2. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall commence on {{START_DATE}} and continue for an initial term of {{INITIAL_TERM}} months. Thereafter, either party may terminate by giving {{NOTICE_PERIOD}} days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach not remedied within 14 days.

## 3. Compensation and Payment

The Client shall pay the Supplier the total consideration of {{CURRENCY}} {{CONTRACT_VALUE}} (the "Contract Value"). Invoices shall be submitted in accordance with the payment schedule agreed in the relevant Statement of Work. Payment shall be made within {{PAYMENT_TERMS}} of receipt of a valid invoice.

## 4. Expenses

The Supplier shall be reimbursed for all reasonable pre-approved expenses incurred in performing the Services, subject to provision of receipts.

## 5. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property rights in any deliverables created by the Supplier under this Agreement shall vest in the Client upon full payment. The Supplier warrants that it has the right to assign such rights.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information disclosed by the other. This obligation shall survive termination for a period of 5 years.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

Neither party excludes liability for death or personal injury, fraud, or any liability that cannot be excluded under English law. Subject to this, each party's total aggregate liability shall not exceed the Contract Value.

## 8. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

## 10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings.
