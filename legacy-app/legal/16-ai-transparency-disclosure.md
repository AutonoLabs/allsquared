## HOW AI IS USED IN YOUR DISPUTE

AllSquared uses artificial intelligence to assist in resolving your dispute. This notice explains how, in plain English.

---

### 1. What the AI does

When you submit a complaint, AllSquared's AI system:

- reads and analyses your complaint, the trader's response, and all supporting documents;
- identifies the key issues in dispute and the applicable consumer law principles;
- drafts a proposed outcome based on that analysis.

The AI processes your information to assist our human ADR Official. It does not make a final decision on your dispute independently.

---

### 2. Who makes the final decision

**A named human ADR Official reviews every proposed outcome before it is sent to you.**

The ADR Official handling your case is: **[Name], [MCIArb / qualification]**

The ADR Official may:
- accept the AI's proposed outcome;
- modify it; or
- substitute their own determination entirely.

All outcomes issued by AllSquared are issued by, and are the responsibility of, the human ADR Official — not the AI system.

This means that AllSquared's process is **not solely automated decision-making** within the meaning of Article 22 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). You are not subject to a decision based solely on automated processing.

---

### 3. Your right to human review

Because a human ADR Official reviews all outcomes, you always have access to a person who can consider your individual circumstances.

If you believe the proposed outcome does not reflect the facts of your case, you may:
- submit additional information or comments before accepting any outcome; or
- request escalation to Tier 2 (human mediation) — see our Procedural Rules.

---

### 4. What data the AI uses

The AI processes the following personal data for the purpose of resolving your dispute:

- your name and contact details;
- details of your complaint and the trader's response;
- documents and evidence you submit;
- relevant transaction data you provide.

**Legal basis:** Processing is necessary for the performance of a task in the public interest (provision of CTSI-approved ADR services) and, where applicable, for the performance of a contract to which you are party.

AllSquared does not use your dispute data to train its AI model without your separate, explicit consent. Dispute data is retained for [3 years] from case closure and then securely deleted, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

---

### 5. AI limitations — what it cannot do

AllSquared's AI system:

- does **not** have access to external databases, court records, or third-party financial information unless you provide them;
- may occasionally misread complex or ambiguous factual situations — this is why human ADR Official review is mandatory;
- does **not** guarantee a particular outcome; its analysis is one input into the ADR Official's determination.

---

### 6. IBA Guidelines on AI in Mediation

AllSquared's use of AI in dispute resolution is designed in accordance with the International Bar Association's *Guidelines on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in Mediation* (June 2025), which require:

- transparency about AI use to all parties (this disclosure);
- human mediator responsibility for all outcomes;
- safeguards against algorithmic bias; and
- data protection compliance.

---

### 7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:
- access the personal data AllSquared holds about you;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion of your data (subject to our legal retention obligations);
- object to processing in certain circumstances;
- lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise these rights, contact: [privacy@allsquared.co.uk]
