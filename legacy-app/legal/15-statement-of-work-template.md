## 13. CHANGE REQUEST PROCEDURE

**Definition of a Change Request:** Any modification to the Scope of Work, Deliverables, timeline, or acceptance criteria that was not specified in the original SOW.

**Procedure:**

1. **Client Requests Change:** Client submits a change request via the AllSquared Platform's Change Request tool (or by email to Freelancer)
2. **Freelancer Estimates:** Freelancer assesses the change and provides:
   - Description of the change and how it impacts the project
   - Estimated additional hours or cost
   - Impact on timeline (does this delay delivery?)
   - Recommendation: approve, decline, or propose alternative
3. **Client Approves/Declines:** Client approves the change (and associated cost/timeline impact) or declines
4. **If Approved:** Freelancer issues a Change Order (amendment to this SOW) documenting:
   - Change description
   - Additional fee or timeline adjustment
   - New delivery date (if affected)
5. **Both Parties Sign:** Both Client and Freelancer sign the Change Order
6. **Work Proceeds:** Freelancer performs the approved change; payment is released upon acceptance of the changed Deliverable

**Note:** No change is binding unless approved in writing via Change Order. Freelancer is not obligated to perform unauthorized changes.

**Examples of Changes That Require a Change Order:**
- "Add a fourth page to the design" (expanded scope)
- "Change the entire color palette" (revision beyond original design direction)
- "Redesign the navigation menu" (scope expansion)
- "Accelerate the timeline by 2 weeks" (resource cost)
- "Create designs in 4 languages instead of English only" (expanded scope)

**Examples of Changes That Are Usually Included (No Change Order):**
- "Adjust the font size on this button by 2px" (minor revision, usually within 1–2 revision rounds)
- "Change this color from blue to green" (minor adjustment to design)
- "Fix the typo on the homepage" (correction, not a change)

## 14. ACCEPTANCE PROCEDURE

**How Acceptance Works:**

1. **Freelancer Delivers:** Freelancer uploads Deliverable(s) to the AllSquared Platform and notifies Client
2. **Client Reviews:** Client has 14 Working Days to review the Deliverable ("**Dispute Window**")
3. **Client Action:**
   - **Accept:** Click "Accept" on the Platform → Escrow funds release to Freelancer (less Platform Fee)
   - **Reject:** Click "Reject" and provide specific feedback on defects → Freelancer has up to 10 Working Days to rework
   - **Silent:** If Client does not Accept or Reject within 14 Working Days, funds are automatically released (Auto-Release)

4. **If Rejected:** Freelancer revises the Deliverable and resubmits. New 14-day Dispute Window begins. This cycle continues until Client accepts.

**Acceptance Criteria** (from Clause 6 above) must be objective and measurable. If acceptance criteria are subjective ("Client must be satisfied"), Client must specify what "satisfaction" means (e.g., "Design reflects brand guidelines; Client CEO approval required").

**Fallback Acceptance Process** (if Client cannot access the Platform):
- Freelancer sends final Deliverable by email
- Client replies with "I accept" or provides rejection feedback
- Email confirmation is valid acceptance
