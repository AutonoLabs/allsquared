## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Policy sets out how AllSquared identifies, discloses, manages, and resolves conflicts of interest that may arise in the course of conducting Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) proceedings under AllSquared's ADR scheme.

1.2 AllSquared is an approved ADR entity under the Alternative Dispute Resolution for Consumer Disputes (Competent Authorities and Information) Regulations 2015 (SI 2015/542), approved by the Chartered Trading Standards Institute (CTSI).

1.3 This Policy applies to:

    (a) all ADR Official(s) appointed by AllSquared to conduct Tier 2 dispute resolution proceedings;
    (b) all staff members involved in Tier 1 (AI-Assisted Facilitation) and Tier 2 proceedings;
    (c) all disputes arising from engagements facilitated through the AllSquared Platform.

1.4 This Policy is publicly available on AllSquared's website and is provided to all parties to a dispute upon initiation of formal ADR proceedings.

---

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **"ADR Official"** means the natural person appointed by AllSquared to conduct Tier 2 ADR proceedings and issue determinations. The ADR Official is not an employee of AllSquared directly engaged in dispute resolution on an ad-hoc basis, but an appointed independent official who satisfies all requirements under Schedule 3 of SI 2015/542.

2.2 **"Conflict of Interest"** means any circumstance that creates, or could reasonably be perceived as creating, a bias or partiality that might affect the ADR Official's independence or impartiality in conducting dispute resolution proceedings. Conflicts include personal, financial, professional, or institutional interests.

2.3 **"Material Conflict"** means a Conflict of Interest that is sufficiently serious that it would prevent the ADR Official from conducting proceedings, either because the Official is unable to act impartially or because a reasonable party would lose confidence in the Official's impartiality.

2.4 **"Related Party"** means any person or entity with a direct personal, family, business, or financial relationship to the ADR Official, including but not limited to: spouse, civil partner, dependent child, business partner, shareholder, or any person with whom the ADR Official has a significant ongoing business or personal relationship.

2.5 **"Structural Conflict"** means a conflict arising from the fact that AllSquared is both the platform operator and the approved ADR entity. This is not treated as a Material Conflict under this Policy because it is inherent to the approved model and is managed through the ring-fenced budget and independence safeguards set out below.

2.6 **"Ring-Fenced Budget"** means a dedicated, separate budget allocation from which the ADR Official's remuneration and all ADR-related costs (administrative staff, facilities, training, compliance) are paid, insulated from AllSquared's commercial profit-and-loss accounts.

## 3. THE STRUCTURAL POSITION: ALLSQUARED AS OPERATOR AND ADR ENTITY

3.1 **Dual Role — Lawfulness Under SI 2015/542:** AllSquared operates both as a marketplace platform (connecting clients and freelancers) and as an approved ADR entity. This dual role is lawful under Schedule 3, paragraph 3(g) of SI 2015/542, which expressly permits a professional or business association to employ or engage ADR officials, provided that the officials' remuneration is not linked to dispute outcomes and that a ring-fenced budget is established.

3.2 **Comparable Models:** This structure follows the established model used by other UK ADR entities, including:

    (a) Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS) — operated by the financial services industry but independent in ADR matters;
    (b) Motor Ombudsman — operated by motor industry association members but independent officials;
    (c) Federation of Master Builders (FMB) — disputes resolved by independent officials funded by member levies;
    (d) ABTA Travel Ombudsman — operated by travel industry but staffed by independent officials.

3.3 **Risk Mitigation — Ring-Fenced Budget:** To manage the structural conflict, AllSquared commits to:

    (a) Ring-fenced, dedicated budget for ADR operations, separate from commercial operations, approved annually by [INDEPENDENT OVERSIGHT BODY];
    (b) ADR Official's remuneration is fixed and not linked to the number of disputes, their outcomes, or Platform revenue;
    (c) ADR Official is appointed for a fixed term (minimum 12 months) and is removable only for cause (incompetence, gross misconduct, breach of confidentiality, loss of impartiality);
    (d) ADR Official has no involvement in Platform's commercial decision-making, pricing, or strategy;
    (e) AllSquared staff involved in Platform operations are excluded from dispute resolution proceedings and decision-making;
    (f) all ADR determinations are published (anonymised) to ensure transparency and consistency;
    (g) AllSquared does not appeal, overturn, or second-guess ADR Official determinations.

3.4 **Published Governance:** This Policy and all ADR Procedural Rules are published at https://allsquared.io/legal/adr-conflict-policy and https://allsquared.io/disputes, and copies are provided to all parties at the outset of proceedings.

## 4. CATEGORIES OF CONFLICT OF INTEREST

4.1 **Personal Conflicts (Case-Specific):** A Personal Conflict arises if the ADR Official:

    (a) knows one or both parties personally (family, friend, acquaintance, social relationship);
    (b) has a spousal, civil partnership, or significant personal relationship with either party;
    (c) has a close family relationship with a shareholder, director, or key manager of either party;
    (d) has been employed by either party in the past 3 years, or holds significant shares in either party;
    (e) has a shared investment or joint business venture with either party;
    (f) has had a significant dispute or acrimonious legal relationship with either party.

**Management:** Replaced immediately by another ADR Official.

4.2 **Financial Conflicts:** A Financial Conflict arises if the ADR Official:

    (a) has a financial interest in the outcome of the dispute (e.g., owns shares in either party, has a bet on the outcome, has a financial entitlement that would increase or decrease as a result of the determination);
    (b) has financial incentives linked to the number of disputes, their outcomes, or Platform commercial performance (ruled out by ring-fenced budget);
    (c) receives payment from either party other than from the ring-fenced ADR budget;
    (d) has significant unpaid debts to or from either party.

**Management:** Replaced immediately by another ADR Official.

4.3 **Professional Conflicts:** A Professional Conflict arises if the ADR Official:

    (a) has provided professional services to either party in the past 3 years (legal, accounting, consulting, design, software development, etc.);
    (b) currently provides services to either party (other than as ADR Official);
    (c) has acted as an expert or witness for either party in the past 5 years;
    (d) is in active competition with either party (e.g., operates a competing freelance marketplace);
    (e) has a significant referral or business relationship with either party.

**Management:** Replaced immediately by another ADR Official.

4.4 **Institutional/Structural Conflict:** A Structural Conflict arises from the fact that AllSquared is both the Platform operator and the ADR entity. Structural Conflicts are inherent to the approved model and are not treated as Material Conflicts, provided that:

    (a) the Ring-Fenced Budget is in place and audited independently;
    (b) the ADR Official has no involvement in Platform commercial decisions;
    (c) determinations are not linked to Platform financial performance;
    (d) the determination is made impartially and published.

**Management:** Structural Conflicts are mitigated by governance safeguards (ring-fenced budget, independence declaration, publication of determinations). They do not trigger official replacement unless a party demonstrates a Material Conflict under sections 4.1, 4.2, or 4.3 above.

4.5 **Reputational Conflict:** A Reputational Conflict arises if the ADR Official:

    (a) has publicly expressed strong views about one party (e.g., criticism in media, published articles, social media);
    (b) has a significant professional disagreement with either party that could affect impartiality;
    (c) is a shareholder or director of an entity in direct competition with either party.

**Management:** The ADR Official must disclose and may be replaced if either party objects reasonably.

## 5. DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS

5.1 **Timing — Initial Disclosure:** Upon appointment, each ADR Official must complete and sign the ADR Official Independence Declaration (Part A — Appointment Declaration), disclosing:

    (a) any known relationships with traders registered on the AllSquared Platform;
    (b) any financial interests in Platform commercial outcomes;
    (c) any prior professional engagements with Platform users;
    (d) confirmation that remuneration is fixed and outcome-independent.

5.2 **Timing — Case-Specific Disclosure:** At the outset of each dispute (before Tier 2 proceedings commence), the ADR Official must complete and sign the ADR Official Independence Declaration (Part B — Case-Specific Declaration), disclosing:

    (a) whether the Official has any known relationship with either party;
    (b) whether the Official has any personal, financial, or professional interest that could affect impartiality;
    (c) confirmation that the Official will conduct the matter impartially and without bias.

5.3 **Ongoing Disclosure Duty:** The ADR Official must disclose any Conflict of Interest that arises during the course of proceedings, including conflicts that come to light after proceedings have commenced.

5.4 **How to Disclose:** Conflicts must be disclosed in writing to AllSquared's Dispute Manager ([disputes@allsquared.io]) and to both parties to the dispute immediately upon discovery. The disclosure must specify:

    (a) the nature of the conflict;
    (b) the relationship or interest that creates the conflict;
    (c) the date on which the conflict was discovered;
    (d) whether the Official believes the conflict is Material or manageable by disclosure.

5.5 **Records:** AllSquared maintains a register of all disclosed conflicts of interest, updated at least annually and made available to the Chartered Trading Standards Institute (CTSI) upon request.

## 6. CONFLICT OF INTEREST PROCEDURE

6.1 **Three-Step Process (aligned to SI 2015/542 Schedule 3, paragraph 4):** When a Conflict of Interest is identified or disclosed, the following procedure applies:

**STEP 1: REPLACEMENT**

6.2 If the ADR Official discloses a Material Conflict or if either party objects to the Official's impartiality based on a disclosed conflict, AllSquared must immediately replace the ADR Official with a different official who has no such conflict. Replacement does not delay the 90-day clock; the new Official takes over the matter and proceeds independently.

6.3 Replacement is not a sanction or fault finding; it is a procedural safeguard to ensure impartiality. The departing Official's reasoning and analysis are not shared with the replacement Official to avoid bias.

**STEP 2: REFERRAL TO ANOTHER ADR ENTITY**

6.4 If AllSquared cannot find a replacement Official without a conflict (e.g., the dispute involves a unique matter that creates conflicts for all available Officials), AllSquared must refer the dispute to another approved ADR entity (e.g., CEDR, the British Academy for Dispute Resolution, or another CTSI-approved mediator). The referral must occur within 5 Working Days of determining that replacement is not possible.

6.5 In the event of referral to another ADR entity, that entity's fees apply (payable by the parties in accordance with AllSquared's Fee Schedule). AllSquared does not charge its own fee for a referred dispute.

**STEP 3: NOTIFICATION, OBJECTION, AND CONSENT**

6.6 If a conflict cannot be remedied by replacement or referral, AllSquared must:

    (a) notify both parties in writing of the specific conflict;
    (b) explain why the conflict is Material and cannot be cured;
    (c) provide both parties with the right to object to the Official continuing;
    (d) continue proceedings only if both parties consent in writing to the Official continuing despite the disclosed conflict.

6.7 Objections must be raised within 10 Working Days of notification. If either party objects and does not consent, the matter is escalated to CTSI (see section 6.8 below).

**ESCALATION TO CTSI**

6.8 If a Material Conflict cannot be resolved through replacement, referral, or consent, AllSquared must notify the Chartered Trading Standards Institute (CTSI) within 5 Working Days, providing full details of:

    (a) the conflict;
    (b) the steps taken to remedy it;
    (c) the reasons for referral or cessation of proceedings;
    (d) both parties' positions.

6.9 CTSI may direct AllSquared to take further steps, including terminating proceedings, requiring independent oversight of the Official, or referring the dispute to an external ADR provider.

## 7. RING-FENCED BUDGET

7.1 **Commitment:** AllSquared commits to maintaining a ring-fenced budget for all ADR-related costs and the ADR Official's remuneration, separate from and independent of AllSquared's commercial operations and profit centres.

7.2 **Composition:** The Ring-Fenced Budget covers:

    (a) ADR Official's fixed salary or retainer fee (outcome-independent);
    (b) administrative staff (dispute manager, case handler);
    (c) office space, technology, communications systems dedicated to ADR;
    (d) training, professional development, and legal updates for the ADR Official;
    (e) compliance, audit, and reporting costs (CTSI reporting, annual activity reports);
    (f) insurance, particularly professional indemnity insurance for the ADR Official;
    (g) external audit and conflict of interest oversight.

7.3 **Segregation:** The Ring-Fenced Budget is:

    (a) funded by a dedicated allocation from AllSquared's overall budget (not derived from Platform commercial revenue);
    (b) managed separately from Platform operations budgets;
    (c) subject to an independent annual audit to confirm segregation and proper use;
    (d) not subject to profit-and-loss targets or cost-cutting measures that would compromise ADR quality.

7.4 **Remuneration — Outcome Independence:** The ADR Official's salary/retainer is:

    (a) fixed and agreed annually;
    (b) not linked to the number of disputes resolved;
    (c) not linked to the outcomes of disputes (e.g., no bonus if determinations favour the Platform, no reduction if determinations favour clients or freelancers);
    (d) not contingent on disputes being resolved quickly or slowly;
    (e) adjusted annually by reference to inflation (RPI/CPI) or by agreed adjustment only.

7.5 **Governance:** The Ring-Fenced Budget is subject to:

    (a) annual review by AllSquared's Board or Audit Committee;
    (b) independent external audit confirming proper segregation and use;
    (c) publication of budget summary in AllSquared's Annual ADR Activity Report;
    (d) inspection by CTSI upon request.
