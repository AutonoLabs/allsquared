## 2. Term

The engagement shall commence on {{START_DATE}} and continue until {{END_DATE}} unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

## 3. Compensation

The Client shall pay the Contractor:

- Total contract value: {{CURRENCY}} {{CONTRACT_VALUE}}
- Day rate: {{CURRENCY}} {{DAY_RATE}} per day (if applicable)

Invoices shall be submitted monthly and paid within 30 days of receipt.

## 4. IR35 Status

The parties agree on the Contractor's IR35 status as determined in accordance with HMRC's off-payroll working rules. The Contractor is responsible for their own tax and National Insurance contributions.

## 5. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property rights in work product created by the Contractor in the course of the engagement shall vest in the Client upon creation and full payment.

## 6. Confidentiality

The Contractor shall keep confidential all proprietary information of the Client and shall not use or disclose such information except as necessary to perform the Services. This obligation shall survive termination.

## 7. Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Client. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership or employment relationship.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately on written notice for material breach not remedied within 14 days.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury, or fraud. Subject to this, the Contractor's total aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
