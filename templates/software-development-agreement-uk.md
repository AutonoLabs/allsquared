## 1. Project Scope

{{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (the "Developer") agrees to develop the software project known as {{PROJECT_NAME}} for {{CLIENT_NAME}} (the "Client"). The project shall utilise the following technology stack: {{TECHNOLOGY_STACK}}.

## 2. Delivery Methodology

Development shall follow an agile methodology with sprint-based delivery. Each sprint shall have a duration of {{SPRINT_DURATION}} weeks. The parties shall agree on sprint goals at the start of each sprint.

## 3. Term and Milestones

Development shall commence on {{START_DATE}}. Milestones and deliverables shall be agreed in the project schedule appended to this Agreement.

## 4. Compensation

The Client shall pay the Developer the total contract value of {{CONTRACT_VALUE}} (the "Fee"). Payment milestones shall be linked to sprint completion and acceptance criteria as set out in the project schedule.

## 5. Intellectual Property Assignment

Upon full payment, the Developer irrevocably assigns to the Client all intellectual property rights in the software, source code, documentation, and related deliverables. The Developer shall execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.

## 6. Warranties

The Developer warrants that the software will conform to the agreed specification for a period of {{WARRANTY_PERIOD}} months from delivery. The Developer does not warrant that the software will be error-free or uninterrupted.

## 7. Maintenance and Support

Post-delivery support and maintenance may be provided under a separate support agreement to be negotiated in good faith between the parties.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Developer shall keep confidential all proprietary information of the Client, including trade secrets, business processes, and customer data.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury, fraud, or breach of IP rights. Subject to this, each party's total liability shall not exceed the Fee paid.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
