## 1. Appointment of Escrow Agent

This Escrow Annexure is entered into between {{CLIENT_NAME}} (the "Client"), {{SUPPLIER_NAME}} (the "Supplier"), and the Escrow Agent. The parties agree that funds deposited under this Annexure shall be held and administered on the terms set out below, effective from {{START_DATE}}.

## 2. Deposit of Funds

The Client shall deposit the total amount of {{CONTRACT_VALUE}} (the "Escrow Funds") into the escrow account within {{FUNDING_DEADLINE_DAYS}} days of the Effective Date of this Annexure.

## 3. Release Conditions

The Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Funds in accordance with the release type selected, upon receipt of written confirmation from both parties, or a valid instruction from the Client.

## 4. Escrow Fee

The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to a fee of {{ESCROW_FEE_PERCENTAGE}}% of the Escrow Funds, which shall be deducted from the funds prior to release to the Supplier.

## 5. Interest

Any interest accruing on the Escrow Funds shall be paid to the Client unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 6. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute between the Client and the Supplier regarding release of funds, the Escrow Agent shall retain the funds until receipt of a court order, arbitral award, or written agreement between the parties.

## 7. Termination

This Annexure shall terminate upon the full release of the Escrow Funds in accordance with Clause 3. Either party may terminate upon 30 days' written notice, subject to any outstanding escrow obligations.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss or damage arising from compliance with a facsimile or electronic instruction reasonably believed to be genuine.

## 9. Governing Law

This Annexure shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
